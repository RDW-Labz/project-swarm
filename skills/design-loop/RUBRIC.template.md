# Rubric — <project> <target>

Score each line 1–10. One sentence of evidence per score, naming the screen.
Edit lines to fit the project; keep 8–10 lines; every line must be judgeable from the captured artifacts.

1. Geometry — rounded cards (12–16px), pill buttons and chips; nothing boxy.
2. Depth — page / section / card read as distinct surfaces; gradients and glow are obvious, not timid.
3. Type — display face has character; codes and numbers unambiguous (0O 1Il 5S 8B).
4. Motion — visible on load and on interaction; calm and complete in reduced-motion capture.
5. Workflow vs baseline — the top 3 user tasks are equal or faster than today; dense where density helps.
6. Colour — palette as briefed; contrast numbers from axe meet AA on body text at the smallest width.
7. Hygiene — no repeated information, no wrapped buttons, numbers formatted, nothing clipped.
8. Product feel — looks like something people want to use, not a template.
