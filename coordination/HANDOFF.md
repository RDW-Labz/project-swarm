- Port: `DEFAULT_CLUSTER_LEDGER_PORT = 9448` (overridable via `SWARM_CLUSTER_LEDGER_PORT` env or local config `clusterLedgerPort`).

- Replay protection is timestamp-window only (no nonce/seen-signature cache yet — see open findings below).
